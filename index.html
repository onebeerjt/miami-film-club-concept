Who we are

A CLUB, NOT
A THEATER.

Miami Moviegoers — aka Miami Film Club — exists to champion the theater experience and connect movie lovers across South Florida. Joining is dead simple: buy your ticket ahead of time and show up. Maybe grab some concessions while you're there.

Nights usually come with pre- or post-screening hangouts — billiards, themed cocktails, costume contests, trivia — because half the fun is talking film with people who get it. Free library screenings, anniversary blowouts with live performances, festival outings: every night is built on one idea — the moviegoers are the movie.
